# اللوائح الأكاديمية لمرحلة البكالوريوس - جامعة بيت لحم (Academic Regulations)

> المصدر: Bethlehem University Undergraduate Catalog 2026-2027، اللوائح الأكاديمية العامة. النص منقول بالإنجليزية كما في الكتالوج، والعناوين العربية ترجمة توضيحية.

## Registration: Credit - الساعة المعتمدة ومدة الفصل

The academic year is divided into two semesters of fifteen weeks each not including final exams, and a summer session of six weeks, not including the examination period. Passing grades at the end of each course give the student credits for his/her course work. Credits are based on the number of hours spent each week in class. One credit hour equals one 50 minute class session per week for 15 weeks.

## Registration: Advising - الإرشاد الأكاديمي

Each student is assigned an advisor to provide advice and assistance in the planning of his/her program. It is the student's responsibility to contact the advisor, to know the requirements of his/her program, and to select the correct courses to fulfill all University, Departmental, Major, and Minor requirements.

## Registration: Pre-registration - التسجيل المبكر

Pre-registration is usually held in October for Spring Semester, March for Fall Semester. The student meets with his/her advisor to review (1) all requirements for Acceptance to Major and/or graduation; (2) courses taken to date; and (3) the list of courses to be offered. The Program of Study (POS) and paradigm tools available in the student portal provide the advisor and the student with the necessary information needed to complete this review. With the advisor's help, the student plans a program of study for the coming semester. To maintain enrollment in the University, a student must take a minimum of 15 credits. The student completes the pre-registration steps online, and submits it. After submission the advisor reviews the registered courses and approves or disapproves the registration as appropriate.

## Registration: Registration Steps - خطوات التسجيل

Registration is done online and takes place each semester just before classes begin on days announced by the Registrar's Office.

1. Step 1: Review pre-registration program. If no course change is necessitated by failure, probation, honors, change of major, or cancellation of courses, skip Step 2 and proceed to Step 3.
2. Step 2: If a course change is necessary the student consults with the advisor about possible changes, and works out a revised course schedule, and submit the changes online.
3. Step 3: Pay tuition and fees in a cooperating bank designated by the Finance Office.

Until Step 3 is completed, the student is not registered for courses, will not appear on class lists, and will not be permitted to attend classes. Late registration incurs a penalty fee, and is permitted only during the first week of classes.

## Registration: Course Cancellation - إلغاء المساقات

The University reserves the right to cancel any course in cases of necessity, such as when the number of students registering is insufficient.

## Registration: Overload - العبء الإضافي

(i) To exceed the full-time load of 17 credits a student must have a semester GPA of 3.00 or better and the approval of his/her advisor and the Office of the Academic Vice President.

(ii) Students, whose approved paradigms require it, may enroll for 18 credits with the approval of the Dean of the Faculty.

## Senior Students Extra Load - العبء الإضافي لطلبة السنة الأخيرة

If upon reaching the end of the Spring Semester of the third year, a student still needs at most 3 credit hours in addition to what the paradigm allows to graduate by the set graduation date, the student is allowed an overload of up to three credit hours in the Summer of their third year, or the Fall or Spring of their fourth year provided the following conditions are met:

1. The student is not on probation at the end of the semester preceding the semester in which an overload is to be taken.
2. The CGPA at the end of the semester preceding the semester in which an overload is to be taken is 2.5 or above.
3. If the additional course is a Major course then the Major GPA must be 2.5 or above in the semester preceding the semester in which an overload is to be taken.
4. If the extra credits are to be taken during the Summer, then it is not allowed that any of the courses be done as independent study.
5. This is a one off opportunity, which means that the student is allowed to take a maximum total of 3 hour extra load only once.

Students in this category and who fulfil the above conditions must complete a request for an overload that is available on-line under the Registrar's Office and must secure the approval of their advisors, chairpersons, and Deans before submitting the request to the Academic Office for final approval.

## Registration: Add/Drop - السحب والإضافة

To change courses the student needs to complete the Add/Drop form, to secure the required approvals, and to keep the completed form with the Registrar's Office. Course changes may occur only during the first week of classes or the first two days of a Summer Session.

## Registration: Prisoners - الطلبة الأسرى

The University will make every attempt to assist any student whose studies have been substantially disrupted due to imprisonment.

## Student Load - العبء الدراسي

To maintain enrollment in the University, all students, except as noted below, must take a minimum of 15 credits. The normal credit hour load is from 15 to 17 credits.

Exceptions:

1. Part-time students take a maximum of 7 credits (or two courses including their associated labs), unless the student has a semester GPA of 2.50 or higher and an overload is approved by the Dean of the Faculty.
2. Students on Probation.
3. Seniors, in their last two semesters, may take a reduced load in order to balance the remaining number of credits between the two semesters.
4. In exceptional circumstances, approved by the Dean of the Faculty, a student may withdraw from one course (and done by the end of the withdrawal period). Such students must still take a minimum of 12 credits.
5. Student Load when taking the Remedial English Course ENGR 103 with 2 credits, which is equivalent to a load of 6 credit hours.

## Student Classification - تصنيف الطلبة حسب السنة

- 1st year or freshmen: have earned fewer than 30 credits.
- 2nd year or sophomores: have earned between 30 and 59 credits.
- 3rd year or juniors: have earned between 60 and 89 credits.
- 4th year or seniors: have earned 90 or more credits.

## Identification - البطاقة الجامعية

Each student will be issued a University identification number and a University identification card upon admission to the University. The student will need his/her identification number whenever s/he has business with the Academic or Financial Offices or with the Library. The University identification card will be required for entrance onto the campus and will be surrendered upon withdrawal from the University.

## Degree Requirements (General) - متطلبات الحصول على البكالوريوس

To receive a Baccalaureate degree from Bethlehem University, a student must successfully complete the following requirements:

1. The University Requirements as prescribed in their majors.
2. Major requirements as listed by the Department with a minimum Grade Point Average (GPA) of 2.00.
3. An approved Minor program (when applicable).
4. A minimum GPA of 2.00 computed on all grades earned at Bethlehem University. Transfer credits are not included.
5. Minimum credit hours for degrees.
6. A minimum of 60 credits at Bethlehem University.
7. All obligations to the various divisions of the University, e.g., Finance Office, Library, Bookstore.

## Student Obligations: Course Work and Final Examination - الواجبات والامتحان النهائي

Course Work: Students are required to fulfill all assignments, tests, reports, experiments, etc. at the time scheduled by the teacher. Major exams will be announced at least three school days before the scheduled date. Any student conflicts must be brought to the teacher's attention within 24 hours of the announcement; late requests for postponement need not be considered by the teacher.

Final Examination: Schedules are posted about two weeks before the end of each semester. Examinations must be taken at the scheduled times. If a final examination is missed because of a serious illness or other legitimate reason beyond the student's control, satisfactory evidence must be presented to the Registrar who in consultation with the teacher of the course, will decide whether or not a make-up examination is justified. No repeat examinations will be given for students who have failed a course.

## Student Obligations: Academic Honesty, Lateness, Change of Program - النزاهة الأكاديمية والتأخر

Academic Honesty: Academic honesty is essential to the integrity of the educational process. Any case of academic dishonesty will be penalized by a failure in the examination, report, etc. or, in serious and/or repeated cases, a failure in the course, or potential dismissal.

Lateness: Students are expected to arrive on time for all scheduled classes and laboratory sessions. Lateness disrupts the learning environment and will affect a student's attendance record. Students are responsible for complying with the University's Tardiness Guidelines, as published on the University Portal.

Change of Program: Whenever a change is made in a student's official program, whether in a course or by withdrawal from the University, the student must obtain, complete and file the appropriate form in the Registrar's Office. Students should have a serious reason for any change and must know that their graduation may be delayed as a result of the change.

## Attendance - الحضور والغياب

Bethlehem University expects its students to attend classes on a regular basis. The maximum number of absences which a student can have and still continue in the course is the number of times that a class meets in a two week period. For example, if a three-credit hour class meets three times each week the maximum number of absences is six. If the class meets twice a week, the maximum number of absences is four. This means that the student can be absent for a maximum of 100 minutes for every credit hour.

Students are responsible for complying with the attendance-related provisions set forth in the General Guidelines for Students, available on the University Portal.

Absent Notices:

1. Student is given the first notice if the number of absences in a given course is equal to the number of meetings of the course in one week.
2. The second and final notice is given when the number of absences in a given course reaches the maximum permissible limit which is twice the number of meetings of the course in one week.
3. Continuing absence, which results in an excess of the allowable number, causes the student to obtain a "WF" grade.

## Grades: Course Grading System - نظام العلامات

To receive credit for a course a student must be regular in attendance, satisfactorily complete all assignments, and secure a passing grade according to the marking system established and announced by the teacher.

| Grade | Percent | Grade Points | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 90-100 | 4.0 | Excellent |
| B+ | 85-89 | 3.5 | Very Good |
| B | 80-84 | 3.0 | Good |
| C+ | 75-79 | 2.5 | Above Average |
| C | 70-74 | 2.0 | Average |
| D+ | 65-69 | 1.5 | Below Average |
| D | 60-64 | 1.0 | Inferior work, but passing |
| F | Below 60 | 0 | Failure |
| WF | - | 0 | Failure: Unofficial Withdrawal |
| W | - | - | Withdrawal without penalty |

Two other grades may be assigned: "P" Pass: credits are earned which are not computed into the student's GPA. "I" Incomplete: a temporary grade which indicates that the student has, for an approved reason, not completed all required course work by the end of the semester and has the possibility to pass the course when all requirements are completed. If the work is not completed by the end of the registration period of the next semester at the latest (Fall, Spring, or Summer) the "I" grade becomes "WF". Grades for the students are on-line after the end of each semester.

## Grades: Honors and Graduation Evaluation - لوائح الشرف وتقدير التخرج

Semester Honors for full-time students (minimum 15 credits), published at the end of each semester: Dean's List: semester GPA of 3.50 to 4.00. Honors List: semester GPA of 3.00 to 3.49.

Graduation Honors are based on the cumulative GPA: Highest Honors: Cumulative GPA of 3.50 to 4.00. Honors: Cumulative GPA of 3.00 to 3.49. In-service students are eligible for graduation honors.

Evaluation of Graduation Averages (Cumulative GPA upon Graduation): Outstanding 3.75 - 4.00; Excellent 3.25 - 3.74; Very Good 2.75 - 3.24; Good 2.25 - 2.74; Average 2.00 - 2.24; Not Qualified for Graduation below 2.00.

Transcripts may be requested from the Office of the Registrar for a fee. Upon fulfilling all requirements for graduation, students are given one transcript free of charge.

## Acceptance to Major - القبول في التخصص

Students are expected to be accepted to a Major when they have earned 40 credits, usually after their third semester. All the following criteria must be met to qualify students for Acceptance to Major (ATM):

1. Minimum overall cumulative GPA of 1.90.
2. Minimum major cumulative GPA of 2.00.
3. Completion of at least 3 courses (9 credits) in the major.

Notes: If a student completes 40 credits by the end of a Summer Session, the Acceptance to Major check is done at the end of the following semester. Students who change their major before ATM will be checked for ATM once they earn 40 credits. If conditions 1 and 2 are met but it was impossible for the student to complete 9 hours in the major the student is given a warning rather than probation. Transfer students who do not satisfy all requirements for ATM at the time of transfer will be checked for ATM once they earn 40 hours including transfer credits.

## Completion of a Minor - التخصص الفرعي

In order to graduate from Bethlehem University, some programs require that the student complete an approved Minor. All Minors are composed of 24-26 credits in a particular field. At the time of pre-registration for the student's third semester at the University s/he must declare a Minor using the Declaration of Minor Form. It is the responsibility of the student, with the assistance of his/her advisor, to select a Minor in a field that the student is qualified to pursue. If, at a later date, the student wishes to change his/her Minor a Change of Minor Form must be completed.

## Change of Major - تغيير التخصص

A. When a Faculty admits a student, it is to a Major offered by the Faculty. The Faculty becomes responsible for the student until s/he (1) graduates; (2) is admitted as a transfer by another Faculty; (3) withdraws or is dismissed from the University.

B. Under certain conditions the student can change his/her Major. Acceptance into a new Major is made according to the approved acceptance guidelines for that Major. To change the major the student has to complete a Change of Major Form from the Registrar's Office securing the approvals and signatures of both the previous and new Department Chairpersons and Deans. The completed form is filed in the Registrar's Office.

C. Change of Major is made only during Registration for the Fall and Spring Semesters. If all involved agree, arrangements can be made at pre-registration, but the decision must be based on the student's projected end-of-semester grade report and resultant status. In any case the Change of Major is effective only during Registration.

D. A student can change Major once before Acceptance to Major and once after Acceptance into a Major. An exception to this may be given to a student upon the recommendation of the Dean if the change of Major is needed to avoid a dismissal.

## Change of Minor - تغيير التخصص الفرعي

A. Acceptance into a new Minor is made according to the approved acceptance guidelines for that Minor. To change the Minor the student has to complete a Change of Minor Form from the Registrar's Office securing the approvals and signatures of both the previous and new Department Chairpersons and Deans. The completed form is filed in the Registrar's Office.

B. Change of Minor is made only during Registration for the Fall and Spring Semesters.

C. A student can change the Minor once. An exception to this may be given to a student upon the recommendation of the Dean if the change of Major is needed to avoid a dismissal.

## Withdrawal from One Course - الانسحاب من مساق

As noted under student load, in exceptional circumstances approved by the Dean and the AVP, a student may drop one course from his/her full-time load. This must be done before the end of the seventh full week of classes. After that time, no official withdrawal from a course can be made.

If done in the first week of the semester, the course will not be recorded on their transcript. If done during the second week the approval of the AVP is needed to not record a grade of W. If done after the second week, a grade of "W" will be assigned for the course.

## Withdrawal from the University - الانسحاب من الجامعة

"W" Grade: Official Withdrawal - Without Penalty.

1. Students may officially withdraw from the University before the seventh full week of classes by completing the appropriate form in the Registrar's Office. If done in the first week of the semester, the courses will not be recorded on their transcript. If done after the first week, grades of "W" will be assigned for all courses.
2. After the seventh full week of classes, official withdrawal from the University with the grade of "W" is possible only if the reasons for withdrawal are beyond the control of the student and approved by the Vice President for Academic Affairs (AVP).
3. Students who officially withdraw are NOT guaranteed readmission to the University. Official withdrawal assures only eligibility to reapply. If readmitted, the student will be subject to the regulations in effect at the time of readmission. Students withdrawing while on their second or third semester on probation will probably not be readmitted. Students who withdraw during their first semester at the University may be considered for readmission for a period of two years after their withdrawal. After that period they must reapply and repeat the Entrance Examinations and, for certain programs, an interview.

## Unofficial Withdrawal (WF) - الانسحاب غير الرسمي

"WF" Grade: Un-official Withdrawal - Failure.

1. Students who have been excessively absent from a course(s) (see Attendance) are considered to have withdrawn unofficially and will be assigned a grade of WF by the Registrar's Office.
2. Students who leave the University, but who do not officially withdraw from the University as noted above, will receive WF grades in all courses and thus be dismissed from the University.

Exception: Students who have enrolled for their first semester at the University and have not attended any classes by the third week are considered no-show students and will be assigned a W grade on their transcript.

## Probation - الإنذار الأكاديمي

If in any Semester (not including Summer) a student falls below the minimum academic requirements of the University and is in danger of being dismissed, a warning notice of "Probation" shows on the student's grade report for that Semester. For Freshman and Sophomore students, this generally means that they are below the criteria necessary to earn Acceptance to Major. For those who have been accepted into a Major, this generally means that they are below the criteria for Graduation. To be in good academic standing the following criteria must be met:

A. Maintaining an overall cumulative GPA (CGPA) according to the following: 0 to 39 credit hours: 1.75; 40 to 70 credit hours: 1.90; 71+ credit hours: 2.00.

B. Earning Acceptance to Major according to the criteria of "Acceptance to Major".

C. Maintaining a major GPA (MGPA) of 2.00 after completing 7 credit hours in the Major.

Specifically, not meeting any of the criteria listed above, warrants Probation.

Students on probation may register for up to 17 credit hours (or the number of credits their paradigm requires) in the semester in which they are on probation. A student on probation may drop a course if s/he deems it necessary to maintain good academic standing as long as the course load does not drop below 12 credit hours.

As Probation indicates that the student is not performing at a minimally acceptable level, it is necessary to limit the number of semesters that a student earns Probation. A student may earn Probation for a maximum of three semesters. This means that a student who earns a fourth semester of Probation will be dismissed from the University.

## Repeating Courses - إعادة المساقات

A. "F" Grade: A student who fails a course must repeat it as soon as possible. The following are conditions which apply for repeating failed courses:

1. If a student fails a Faculty/Major required course repeated for the second time, the student will be dismissed from the University unless he/she can earn acceptance into another Faculty/Major, as applicable.
2. A University required course can be repeated three times. If a student repeats a university required course for the third time and fails it, the student will be dismissed from the University.
3. An elective course may or may not be repeated, as the student may choose to fulfill his/her elective requirements by taking a different elective course.

B. "D/D+" Grade: Upon approval of the Vice President for Academic Affairs, a third- or fourth-year student may repeat a maximum of two required courses with D or D+ grades if this is needed to satisfy the graduation requirement of a cumulative GPA of 2.00 or/and a Major GPA of 2.00.

C. GPA: When a course is repeated, both grades will be recorded, but only the more recent of the two will be computed in the GPA.

## Dismissal - الفصل من الجامعة

The University reserves the right to dismiss any student who does not maintain a satisfactory academic record or whose conduct or influence is deemed undesirable. Specifically, the following will warrant Dismissal:

1. Failure of a University requirement for a third time.
2. Failure of a Faculty requirement for the second time unless a change of Faculty is approved, taking into account the change of Major regulations.
3. Failure of a major course for the second time unless a change of Major is approved while taking into account change of Major regulations.
4. Failure of a minor course for the second time unless a change of Minor is approved.
5. Reaching the maximum number of semesters on Probation.
6. Failing all courses in the first semester of study.
7. Serious violation of academic or disciplinary regulations.
8. Non-fulfillment of financial obligations.
9. Submitting false information on the Admission application or any other official documents.

Notes: A student is allowed to change major once before and once after "Acceptance to Major". Towards the end of the first year, every student whose program of study requires a Minor must apply for acceptance to Minor. Once accepted in a Minor, a student may change the Minor only once.

Exception: The Academic Vice President, upon the recommendation of the Dean and Chairperson, may authorize an exception to specific academic regulations for students who have completed at least 80% of the total number of credit hours required for graduation. In particular, in case of an academic dismissal the student may be granted one additional semester on probation before a final dismissal.

## Readmission - إعادة القبول

A student dismissed for academic reasons can apply to rejoin the University after at least one semester from the date of dismissal. The student can only be admitted into the program from which she/he was dismissed provided s/he can remove the causes of dismissal by the end of the semester of readmission.

## Independent Study - الدراسة المستقلة

Independent Study courses are available only to Seniors who are one course short of fulfilling all graduation requirements and this course is not being scheduled on a regular basis prior to graduation. There must be a teacher-student contract (indicating the schedule of meetings, the units of material to be covered, and the methods of evaluation) signed by and approved by the Vice President for Academic Affairs.

## Graduation - التخرج وحفل التخرج

Degrees and Diplomas are awarded at the annual Commencement in early summer to all students who fulfill requirements for graduation during the preceding year. Students who complete requirements in August or January may obtain their certificates from the Dean and transcripts at that time from the Registrar's Office, and participate in the summer ceremony if they wish. Only students who have fulfilled all requirements and satisfied all obligations to the University will be permitted to participate in the Graduation Ceremony.

## Student Rights and Grade Appeals - حقوق الطالب والاعتراض على العلامات

Students have the right to appeal an academic decision, to have grievances considered, to seek alleviation of student problems, to privacy and protection of their school records, and to suggest ways to improve the University's academic life. Students have the right to have all graded material returned and to review their final exams. Any questions or appeals must first be addressed to the teacher and must be made within 48 hours. In the case of final examinations the appeal must be made within three school days after the distribution of Grade Reports. If the teacher is not available, the appeal must be made to the Chairperson or Dean. If the matter cannot be settled between the teacher and student, the student may appeal to the Department Chairperson/Dean. Academic questions are not subject to popular student vote. In all academic matters, the Vice President for Academic Affairs is the final authority.

The University reserves the right to add, withdraw, or otherwise modify its degree and diploma programs, courses of study, or course content as described within this catalog.
