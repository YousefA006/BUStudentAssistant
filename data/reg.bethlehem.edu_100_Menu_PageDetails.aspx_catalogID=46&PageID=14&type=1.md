## Identification

Each student will be issued a University identification number and a University identification card upon admission to the University.  The student will need his/her identification number whenever s/he has business with the Academic or Financial Offices or with the Library.  The University identification card will be required for entrance onto the campus and will be surrendered upon withdrawal from the University.
