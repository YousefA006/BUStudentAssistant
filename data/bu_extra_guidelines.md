# إرشادات إضافية لطلبة جامعة بيت لحم وبرنامج هندسة البرمجيات (Additional Guidelines)

> المصدر: الإرشادات العامة للطلبة (General Guidelines for Students) على بوابة جامعة بيت لحم، وصفحة برنامج هندسة البرمجيات في الكتالوج 2026-2027، كما تحقق منها الفريق. وعند أي تعارض، تُقدَّم اللوائح الأكاديمية في الكتالوج.

## Tardiness Policy - سياسة التأخر

Tardiness: a 10-minute limit applies to 8:00 AM classes only. Three tardy instances count as one absence.

## Excused Absences - الغياب بعذر

Excused absences require the official Excused Absence Form, submitted at least 2 weeks in advance, along with proper documentation.

## Absences for Classes Meeting Once a Week - الغياب لمساق يلتقي مرة أسبوعيًا

For a course that meets once a week, the maximum number of unexcused absences is 2 (the number of meetings in a two-week period). Exceeding the permissible limit results in a WF grade.

## Academic Integrity and AI Use - النزاهة الأكاديمية واستخدام الذكاء الاصطناعي

Academic dishonesty, plagiarism, unauthorized AI use, or cheating in examinations results in a grade of zero (0) for that exam. Supplementary evaluations or oral re-assessments may be required if cheating is strongly suspected.

## Acceptance to Major: English Requirement for Software Engineering - شرط الإنجليزي للقبول في تخصص هندسة البرمجيات

In addition to the general Acceptance to Major criteria (40 credits, cumulative GPA 1.90, major GPA 2.00, and at least 3 courses / 9 credits in the major), Software Engineering students must complete the English language requirements of their track:

- Regular English students: ENGL120 and ENGL121.
- Intermediate English students: ENGR102, ENGL120 and ENGL121.
- Intensive English students: ENGR101, ENGR102 and ENGL120.

## Program Learning Outcomes (PLOs) - مخرجات التعلم لبرنامج هندسة البرمجيات

- Knowledge: understand software engineering ideas, requirements analysis, system design, and emerging ICT technologies.
- Skills: write clean code, execute testing and debugging, solve complex system problems, and work effectively in teams.
- Attitudes: professional responsibility, respect for intellectual property, and commitment to lifelong learning.

## Total Credits Including Intensive English - مجموع الساعات لطلبة الإنجليزي المكثف

Software Engineering requires 131 credit hours. Intensive English students take an additional 2 credits in English (ENGR103), for a total of 133 credit hours.
