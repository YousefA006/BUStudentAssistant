### Program Learning Outcomes

## Knowledge

## 1\. Understand and solve scientific (Math and Physics) and engineering     problems using simulation tools.

## 2. Read and understand commercial software package-handbooks.

## 3\. Understand Mathematical language reasoning hypothesizing, questioning   and logical and Mathematical thinking.

## 4\. Write codes for scientific computing.

## Skills

## 1. Participate in cutting edge computer-based research in Math and Science.

## 2\. Modify and customize available commercial software towards own specific needs by adding user-defined subroutines to it.

## 3\. Transfer the mathematical and physics knowledge to other courses and to real life problems.

## 4\. Plan, execute and report the results of a complex extended experiment or investigation in Physics.

## Attitudes

## 1. Appreciate the contribution of Mathematics and Science in solving real life problems.

## 2\. Work independently and within a team with confidence.
