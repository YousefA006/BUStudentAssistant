### Degree Requirements

To receive a Bachelor’s  degree from Bethlehem University a student must complete:

1. All requirements as listed below

2. A minimum Major Grade Point Average (GPA) of 2.00.

3. A minimum cumulative GPA of 2.00 computed on all grades earned at Bethlehem University. Transfer credits are not included.

4. Minimum total credit hours for degree.


| Degree Requirements | Number of credits |
| --- | --- |
| University Requirement | 29 |
| Faculty Requirement | 15 |
| Major Requirement | 72 |
| Major Elective | 15 |
| **Total number of credits** \*: | **131** |

\*Intensive English Students take an additional 2 credits in English (ENGR 103).
