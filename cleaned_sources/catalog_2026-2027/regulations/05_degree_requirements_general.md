## Degree requirements

To receive a Baccalaureate degree from Bethlehem University, a student must successfully complete the following requirements:
1. The University Requirements as prescribed in their majors
2. Major requirements as listed by the Department with a minimum Grade Point Average (GPA) of 2.00.
3. An approved Minor program (when applicable)
4. A minimum GPA of 2.00 computed on all grades earned at Bethlehem University. Transfer credits are not included.
5. Minimum credit hours for degrees (listed on each program's own Degree Requirements page).
6. A minimum of 60 credits at Bethlehem University.
7. All obligations to the various divisions of the University, e.g., Finance Office, Library, Bookstore.
